Values

How we decide on every project

  • Evidence before equipment — test and assess before recommending RO, softener or ETP capacity.
  • Right-sizing over upselling — a smaller, correct system beats a large, stressed one.
  • Accountability after install — commissioning is the start of the relationship.
  • Plain-language engineering — committees and operators deserve understandable proposals.
  • Local responsiveness — Shela-based support for west Ahmedabad’s daily water issues.
  • Respect for water — recovery, recycling and responsible discharge where feasible.
