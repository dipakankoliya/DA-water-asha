FAQ

Filtration design questions

Do I always need a softener before RO in Ahmedabad?
Often yes when hardness is elevated, but not blindly. We check feed hardness, recovery targets and existing pre-treatment before adding resin stages — oversizing softeners wastes salt and under-sizing kills membranes.
Can you redesign an existing society filter room?
Yes. Many Shela and Bopal societies have mismatched vessels, stuck valves or media that was never graded correctly. We audit hydraulics and chemistry, then propose repair, re-media or staged replacement.
What information do you need for a design quote?
Recent water test results if available, source type (bore / AMC / tanker mix), peak daily demand, plant-room photos and whether the filtrate feeds RO, drinking or only plumbing comfort.
Do you design only, or also supply and install?
Both. Some clients want a vendor-neutral scheme for tendering; most prefer DA Water to design, supply, install and maintain under one accountable contract.
How long does a typical filtration design take?
After sampling, a residential or society scheme is usually ready within a few working days. Commercial and industrial trains with complex recovery or footprint constraints take longer — we set timelines in the proposal.
