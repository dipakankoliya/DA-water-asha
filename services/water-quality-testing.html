Is a handheld TDS reading enough?
It is a useful start, not the whole story. Hardness and other factors often decide softeners vs RO. We explain what else you may need.
Do you test industrial process water?
Yes — we align testing with process or discharge questions and may recommend accredited lab panels when required.
Can testing credit toward a project?
Ask when you book; assessment fees are often adjusted against project value.
How do I prepare?
Note water source (bore/municipal/tanker), recent issues, and any prior reports.
Next step after testing?
Often purification, RO, or filtration design.
