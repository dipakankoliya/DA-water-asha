FAQ

Installation & AMC questions

Can you maintain a plant someone else installed?
Yes. We audit first — valves, membranes, media age, electrical safety — then propose an AMC that matches the plant’s real condition, not a generic checklist.
How often should Ahmedabad RO systems be serviced?
Most commercial and society plants need quarterly preventive visits at minimum, with pre-filter changes more often when turbidity spikes. High-TDS bore feeds may need tighter CIP and membrane monitoring in summer.
Do AMC contracts include spare parts?
Scopes vary. Some cover labour and routine filters only; others bundle membranes, UV lamps or softener salt planning. We state inclusions clearly before you sign.
Will installation disrupt a residential society?
We plan around tank filling and peak morning demand, use temporary bypasses where feasible, and keep secretaries informed so residents are not left without water without notice.
