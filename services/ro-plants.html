Why RO fails early

Most RO problems start before the membrane

High TDS bore sources, skipped softening, weak antiscalant discipline and optimistic recovery claims shorten membrane life. We size pre-treatment as carefully as the RO skid itself.

  • Capacity matched to real peak demand (not brochure averages)
  • Pre-treatment for hardness, turbidity and chlorine where needed
  • Commissioning with baseline logs your team can repeat
  • AMC that protects recovery through summer load
