FAQ

Consulting questions

Is consulting only for large industrial plants?
No. Many of our audits are for residential societies and commercial buildings where committees are stuck between conflicting RO or softener quotes. Scale the engagement to the decision you need to make.
Will you recommend only DA Water products?
We disclose when we can also supply and install. Consulting deliverables still rank repair, reconfigure and replace options on merit — including staying with another vendor when that is sensible.
How long does a typical society audit take?
Most west Ahmedabad society audits are a focused site visit plus a written findings note within a few working days. Complex industrial reviews take longer depending on sampling and documentation access.
Can you help us challenge an existing AMC vendor?
Yes. We compare promised visits and water results against what the plant actually shows — then you decide whether to renegotiate, switch or invest in upgrades.
Do you advise on Gujarat ETP/STP expectations?
We provide practical, treatment-focused guidance and documentation checklists. Formal legal or environmental consultancy of record, where required, remains with your appointed professionals.
What should I prepare before the first call?
Location, water source, recent lab reports if any, photos of the plant room, current pain points (taste, scale, odour, downtime) and copies of competing quotes if you have them. Or simply call +91 9913654239.
