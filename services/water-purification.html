FAQ

Purification questions

Is RO always required for Ahmedabad homes?
Not always. If hardness is the main complaint and TDS is moderate, softening may be enough for plumbing comfort. Drinking goals often still benefit from RO — we test before deciding.
Can you upgrade an existing under-sink RO?
Yes. We check pre-filters, membrane life, storage hygiene and whether a central system would serve the home better.
Do you handle society common purification plants?
Yes — capacity planning, installation and AMC for residential societies across west Ahmedabad.
