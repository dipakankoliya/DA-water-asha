## 1. Business Analysis

### Industry
Water treatment in Gujarat is driven by hard groundwater, elevated TDS in many bore sources, rapid residential/commercial growth in west Ahmedabad (Shela, Bopal, SG Road), and industrial compliance pressure (ETP/STP, recycling). Buyers span homeowners, society committees, F&B/commercial kitchens, healthcare, hospitality, and manufacturing plants.

### Target Audience
| Segment | Intent | Decision drivers |
|--------|--------|------------------|
| Homeowners / villas | Safe drinking water, less scaling | Trust, clarity, after-sales |
| Housing societies | Common RO/STP/AMC | Committee consensus, AMC reliability |
| Commercial (offices, retail, kitchens) | Continuous supply, taste, hygiene | Uptime, capacity sizing |
| Industrial / process | Process water + discharge norms | Engineering depth, documentation |
| Consultants / facility managers | Vendor who won’t disappear | Responsiveness, reporting |

### Local Market (Ahmedabad)
- West Ahmedabad residential boom: Shela, Bopal, South Bopal, Science City corridor.
- Commercial density: SG Highway, Prahlad Nagar, Satellite.
- Industrial demand: Vatva, Naroda, Kathwada and surrounding estates need RO, softener, ETP.
- Seasonal summer load spikes membrane stress and service calls.

### Competitors (representative)
MG RO Technology, AAIMA Water, Aquashakti, Swati Water, Ankit Sizing — mostly industrial-RO/ETP focused catalogue sites. Opportunity: clearer residential+commercial storytelling, Shela local SEO, stronger trust/AMC narrative, conversion UX.

### Customer Pain Points
- Guesswork quotations without testing  
- Scaling, bitter taste, high TDS  
- Failed membranes from poor pre-treatment  
- Unreliable AMC / no logs  
- Confusion between softener vs RO  
- Wastewater non-compliance anxiety  

### Psychology & Buying Intent
High-involvement purchase. Buyers seek **risk reduction**: proof of local presence, process clarity, written AMC, before/after parameters. Commercial/industrial buyers need **authority + documentation**; residential needs **empathy + simplicity**.

### Local SEO Opportunities
- “water treatment plant Ahmedabad”, “RO plant manufacturer Ahmedabad”, “ETP STP Shela”, “water softener Bopal”, “water testing Ahmedabad”, service+area pages, Google Business Profile optimization for Shela.

### USPs / Advantages
1. End-to-end: test → design → install → AMC  
2. Multi-segment fluency (home to industry)  
3. Local Shela base with fast west-Ahmedabad response  
4. Compliance-aware wastewater mindset  
5. Plain-language engineering (committee & plant-manager friendly)

### Trust Factors
Phone-first accessibility, physical address, process transparency, case studies, AMC retention narrative, schema/NAP consistency.

### Marketing Angle
**“Ahmedabad water, correctly engineered.”** Not cheapest catalogue — right-sized systems with aftercare.

## 16. UI/UX Design Guidelines

- **Layout:** Premium corporate-aquatic; generous whitespace; section rhythm 4–6.5rem.  
- **Typography:** Fraunces (display) + Outfit (UI/body).  
- **Palette:** Deep `#05353c`, Primary `#0a6570`, Accent brass `#b8956c`, Mist `#eef5f6`, Foam `#f7fbfb`.  
- **Buttons:** Brass primary CTAs; teal secondary; outline on dark heroes.  
- **Cards:** Avoid decorative cards; use bordered interaction tiles / hairline dividers.  
- **Motion:** Rise-in hero, scroll reveal, counter animation, logo marquee (respect `prefers-reduced-motion`).  
- **Mobile:** Sticky bottom Call + Quote bar; floating WhatsApp/Call.  
- **Not used:** Purple gradients, cream/terracotta cliché, pill spam, hero badge overlays.

## 17. Developer Instructions (summary)

| Item | Spec |
|------|------|
| Stack | Static HTML/CSS/JS (no build required) |
| Shared chrome | `#site-header` / `#site-footer` injected by `js/main.js` |
| CSS | `css/styles.css` design tokens + components |
| Forms | `data-redirect="thank-you.html"`; wire to Formspree/CRM later |
| Images | WebP/AVIF, lazy load below fold, width/height attributes |
| Performance | Inline critical not required initially; defer JS; compress images |
| A11y | Focus states, aria labels on floats, summary FAQ, contrast on teal |
| URLs | Lowercase kebab-case; trailing structure as in sitemap |
| Canonical | `https://www.dawater.in/...` |

- **Purpose:** Brand + primary conversion

- **Tablet/Mobile:** Reduced min-height; stacked CTAs full width
