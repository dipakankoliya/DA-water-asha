Buying guide

How to choose an RO plant for societies and commercial sites

Catalogue LPH ratings sell plants. Peak-hour demand, feed TDS, and pretreatment decide whether those plants survive Ahmedabad’s first monsoon silt spike.

RO (reverse osmosis) plants remove dissolved salts through membranes. In Ahmedabad—especially western corridors like Shela, Bopal, and SG Highway—feed water often combines high TDS with hardness and fine silt. Choosing a plant means designing a system, not picking a sticker capacity.

1. Start with peak demand, not daily average

Societies draw heavily in morning and evening windows. Hotels spike during banquets. Size for the peak hour plus storage strategy. A 500 LPH plant that runs 20 hours on paper may still starve showers at 7 a.m. if tanks and fill rates are wrong. Our RO plant service begins with occupancy and use mapping.

2. Insist on feed water data

Ask for TDS, hardness, iron, turbidity/SDI risk, and any chlorine residual. Without numbers, membrane warranty conversations are theatre. Use lab testing before tendering.

3. Pretreatment is not optional here

Multimedia filtration, antiscalant dosing, and cartridge protection keep membranes alive. Skipping them to “save cost” usually returns as emergency CIP and early replacements—see filtration design.

4. Talk honestly about recovery and reject

High recovery looks attractive until scale forms. Gujarat hardness chemistry sets practical limits. Plan reject reuse for flushing or gardening only where layout and permissions allow—never as an afterthought.

5. Demand instrumentation and access

Online product TDS, pressure gauges, and CIP access turn operators into problem-solvers. Basement plant rooms need clear valves and sample ports, not spaghetti piping.

6. Price the AMC, not just the skid

Ask what visits include, which consumables are extra, and how emergencies are handled. Society AMC along western Ahmedabad is where plants succeed or silently fail—details under installation & AMC.

Quick buyer checklist

  • Peak LPH and storage volume agreed in writing
  • Pretreatment vessels sized for silt, not brochure minimums
  • Antiscalant programme matched to hardness
  • Redundancy plan for 24×7 sites
  • Commissioning TDS log and operator training included
  • AMC SLA with response expectations
