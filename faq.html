Frequently asked questions

Straight answers from DA Water on hard water, RO, ETP/STP, testing, and maintenance across Ahmedabad and Gujarat.

Why is water in Ahmedabad often hard and high in TDS?
Much of the city relies on groundwater and mixed municipal supply. Borewells in Shela, Bopal, and surrounding west-side localities commonly show elevated hardness and TDS from mineral-rich aquifers. Treatment must follow measured values.
Do I need both a softener and an RO system?
Often yes for Gujarat homes. Softeners reduce hardness (scale on fixtures and heaters). RO lowers dissolved salts for drinking. We test first and recommend softener-only, RO-only, or hybrid based on your numbers and usage.
What TDS range usually points toward RO for drinking?
Many households choose RO when TDS stays consistently high for taste and drinking preference, alongside other lab parameters. We never size an RO plant without sampling and understanding daily demand.
Can DA Water support GPCB compliance for ETP or STP?
Yes. We design and upgrade effluent and sewage systems with clear sampling points and documentation habits aligned to consent conditions — especially relevant for Vatva, Naroda, and other industrial clusters.

We are based near Club O7 Road, Shela. Most west-Ahmedabad assessments are within 24–48 hours; industrial surveys in east Ahmedabad are typically within 2–3 working days.
Do you test water before quoting a system?
Yes. Field checks and lab-backed analysis for TDS, hardness, pH, microbes, and industrial parameters inform every serious quotation. Guesswork creates failed plants.
What does an AMC include?
Preventive visits, filter and media care, membrane health checks, chemical dosing review, and performance logging. Residential, commercial, and industrial scopes differ — ask for a tailored schedule.
Can you fix an existing RO or ETP that underperforms?
Yes. Audits often reveal pretreatment gaps, wrong recovery settings, exhausted media, or operator issues. We propose targeted upgrades before recommending full replacement.
Do you install commercial RO for societies and hotels?
Yes — capacity planned for peak occupancy, kitchens, and storage, with AMC options for societies along SG Road and hospitality sites across Ahmedabad.
How does summer scarcity change design?
Feed quality and availability can swing in peak summer. We plan recovery, storage, and mixed sources (municipal, borewell, tanker) that are common in Ahmedabad.
