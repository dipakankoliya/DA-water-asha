Bopal FAQ

Hard water questions we hear weekly

Is hard water really that common in Bopal?
Elevated hardness is a frequent finding in Bopal and South Bopal bore and mixed supplies. Exact numbers vary by society and season — which is why we test before recommending softeners or RO.
Will a softener alone fix drinking water taste?
Usually not. Softeners address scale-forming minerals for plumbing comfort. Taste, TDS and microbiological goals often need separate purification stages. We design both loops deliberately.
Can you help a South Bopal society whose softener barely works?
Yes. Channelled resin, wrong regen settings and undersized vessels are common. We diagnose, re-media or redesign, then offer AMC so the fix lasts past the first AGM.
How soon can you visit Bopal from Shela?

Do flat owners need their own RO if the society has one?
It depends on product-water quality at the flat, storage hygiene and distribution distance. We check end-tap results — not only the plant-room gauge — before advising extra point-of-use units.
