SG Highway FAQ

Questions from kitchens and facility teams

Can you install without shutting the kitchen for a full day?
Often yes. We plan piping and changeovers around prep windows, use temporary supply where feasible, and agree a cutover plan with the chef or F&B manager before tools come in.
How do you size RO for a busy restaurant?
We map beverage, cooking, ice and staff drinking demand by hour — not a single daily total — then add storage and recovery buffers for lunch and dinner peaks.
Do you offer AMC for offices in multi-tenant towers?
Yes. We coordinate access with facility management, schedule visits outside core meeting hours when possible, and keep logs facility teams can archive.
Our existing RO cannot keep up after 12 noon — can you help?
A capacity and storage audit usually finds undersized membranes, weak pre-treatment or tank strategy. We recommend repair, add-on capacity or replacement only when justified.
