Projects that prove the process

Four detailed engagements — from Shela homes to Vatva and Naroda industrial sites — showing how DA Water diagnoses, designs, and delivers.

Residential · Shela

Villa hard-water recovery: softener + drinking RO

A Club O7 Road villa struggled with scale on fixtures, cloudy glassware, and drinking water above preferred TDS from borewell supply.

  • Challenge: High hardness with elevated TDS; previous under-sink RO alone did not stop whole-home scale
  • Approach: Full-house softener sized to occupancy, point-of-use RO for kitchen, sediment pretreatment
  • Result: Visible scale reduction within days; stable drinking TDS; AMC for salt and filter cadence

Commercial · Bopal society

Central RO redesign for a housing society

Residents reported metallic taste and frequent membrane failures on an aging central plant serving multiple towers.

  • Challenge: Inadequate pretreatment and recovery settings mismatched to summer feed quality
  • Approach: Water audit, sand/carbon upgrade, antiscalant discipline, correctly sized membranes, operator training
  • Result: Fewer complaint tickets, longer membrane life, documented AMC visits for the committee

Industrial · Vatva GIDC

ETP upgrade for COD reduction & audit readiness

A manufacturing unit faced inconsistent outlet quality and needed clearer sampling discipline ahead of regulatory scrutiny.

  • Challenge: Variable influent, aging aeration, weak sludge handling, incomplete logbooks
  • Approach: Process review, equalization improvements, biological stage tuning, monitoring points, operator SOPs
  • Result: More stable COD performance and documentation habits aligned with GPCB inspection expectations

Industrial · Naroda

STP capacity and compliance for expanding workforce

Headcount growth pushed an existing STP beyond reliable treatment, risking consent conditions during renewal.

  • Challenge: Hydraulic overload and inconsistent disinfection
  • Approach: Capacity reassessment, process upgrades, dosing automation, commissioning with EHS team
  • Result: Stable treated sewage quality and a clear AMC plan for ongoing GPCB-facing reporting
